I finally did my laundry today. I’d been putting it off all week, and this morning I realised I had no clean socks left. So that was my sign.

I put a load on, made some coffee, and sat down for a bit. I always tell myself I’ll clean the kitchen while the washing machine’s running, but today I just didn’t feel like it.

A friend called while I was waiting, and we ended up talking for almost an hour. Nothing special—just work, dinner plans, that kind of thing. It was nice to have a chat without having to be anywhere.

Anyway, I got the washing out and hung everything up. And now it’s all dry, sitting on my bed, waiting for me to put it away.

That’s the part I always leave until later. Washing it? Fine. Hanging it up? Fine. Putting it away? Somehow, that feels like a whole other job.

But at least I’ve caught up on the laundry. Well, almost. I’ll probably move the clothes onto the chair when I go to bed.
